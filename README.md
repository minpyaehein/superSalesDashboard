 This is my first Power BI dashboard using the Superstore Sales dataset 📊

This project helped me understand how raw sales data can be converted into meaningful business insights through visualization and dashboard design.

Key Highlights:
• Sales & Profit Analysis with Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
• Monthly Sales Trends & Peak Performance Tracking
• Country-wise Revenue Distribution (Treemap Analysis)
• Category & Segment-wise Performance Breakdown
• Interactive Regional Filtering

Tools & Skills Used:
Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
Power BI (Data Modeling & Table Joining)
Basic DAX (YoY Growth Calculations)
Dashboard Design & Data Visualization

Some insights I found interesting:
• The Consumer segment drives the most business, accounting for nearly half of all sales (49.6%).
• Chairs and Phones are the top revenue-generating products in the store.
• China and India stand out as the highest revenue-generating countries in the region.
